Learn Gsap ScrollTrigger

The scrollTrigger() method is used to create scroll-based animations that are triggered and controlled by the user's scroll actions.

ScrollTrigger allows developers to easily link animations to the scroll position of the webpage, enabling effects such as parallax scrolling, pinning elements during scroll, and triggering animations when specific elements enter or leave the viewport.

Unlike traditional animations that play based on time, ScrollTrigger animations respond dynamically to the user's scrolling behavior, providing a more interactive and engaging experience.

ScrollTrigger also supports features such as relative positioning, overlapping animations using time offsets, labels for navigation, and nesting of other timelines, making them ideal for complex animation sequences.
